# Fossil Data from the Dahonggou section
  
The original data was published in Han et al.(2026). The dataset was recompiled and transformed here for evaluating the performance of ML models

## References

Han, F., Yang, T.L., Liang, Y., Zhang, K.X., Ji, J.L., Song, B.W., 2026. Early Oligocene paleoclimatic reconstruction of the Qaidam Basin (northern Qinghai-Xizang Plateau): Insights from sporopollen and n-alkane records. Palaeoworld 35(4), 201125.
